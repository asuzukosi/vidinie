Frequently Asked Questions.

Find answers to common questions about our video creation platform

What file formats do you support?
We support PDF documents and website URLs. Our AI can extract content from both sources and transform them into engaging videos with professional transitions and effects.
How long does it take to create a video?
Most videos are generated in 2-5 minutes depending on the length of your content. Our AI processes your content, selects the best visuals, adds transitions, and exports your final video automatically.
Can I customize the videos?
Yes! You can customize templates, colors, fonts, music, transitions, and add your own branding. Professional and Enterprise plans include advanced customization options.
What video formats can I export?
You can export videos in MP4, MOV, WebM, and GIF formats. We support resolutions from 720p HD up to 4K Ultra HD on Professional and Enterprise plans.
